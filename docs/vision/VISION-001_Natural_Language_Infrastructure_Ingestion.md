# VISION-001 — Natural-Language Infrastructure Ingestion

**Status:** Vision  
**Normative:** No  
**Implementation scope:** Future

## 1. Purpose

This document describes a future direction for Persiqa: enabling infrastructure
knowledge described in natural language to be transformed into a structured
Persiqa representation and, ultimately, into the Canonical Knowledge Model
(CKM).

This document is non-normative. It does not define current implementation
requirements, language semantics, validation requirements, or conformance
requirements.

## 2. Scope Boundary

Natural-language ingestion is intentionally outside the initial implementation
scope.

The current Persiqa system SHALL be usable without natural-language processing.
Structured data, APIs, forms, or other deterministic input mechanisms MAY
populate the semantic model directly.

Natural-language ingestion is a future ingestion and authoring capability. It
SHALL NOT be a prerequisite for the Core model, CKM, validation, persistence,
reasoning, or serialization.

## 3. Intended Future Flow

The intended future architecture is:

```text
Natural-language source
        |
        v
Semantic extraction / interpretation
        |
        v
Persiqa structured representation
        |
        v
Semantic validation
        |
        v
Canonical Knowledge Model (CKM)
```

The natural-language layer is an input adapter. The CKM remains the authoritative
semantic representation.

## 4. Role of the Persiqa DSL

The Persiqa DSL is a deterministic, human-readable authoring and interchange
representation.

In a future natural-language workflow, an extraction system MAY produce PDS
rather than constructing the CKM directly:

```text
Natural language
       |
       v
Extraction
       |
       v
PDS
       |
       v
Semantic interpretation
       |
       v
CKM
```

This keeps natural-language interpretation separate from the canonical
semantic model.

PDS remains useful independently of natural-language ingestion. It MAY be
written by humans, generated by software, or used as an interchange format.

## 5. Grounding and Non-Invention

A future natural-language ingestion system SHOULD distinguish between facts
explicitly supported by source material and interpretations introduced by the
extraction process.

It SHOULD NOT silently invent infrastructure facts that are not supported by
the source.

Where the source is uncertain, ambiguous, conditional, or hypothetical, the
result SHOULD preserve that distinction rather than presenting the
interpretation as established infrastructure fact.

## 6. Provenance

A future implementation SHOULD preserve provenance between extracted
knowledge and its source material.

The intended relationship is:

```text
Source document
      |
      v
Source passage / observation
      |
      v
Extracted assertion
      |
      v
Persiqa representation
      |
      v
CKM
```

Provenance is a future capability and is not currently required by the Core
model solely to support this vision.

## 7. Human Validation

Natural-language extraction SHOULD support human validation before uncertain
or consequential knowledge is treated as authoritative infrastructure state.

A possible future workflow is:

```text
Source
  |
  v
Extraction
  |
  v
Candidate knowledge
  |
  v
Human review
  |
  v
CKM
```

The exact review workflow is intentionally left open.

## 8. Relationship to the Current Architecture

The Core ontology, CKM, identity model, ownership model, validation model,
serialization model, and reasoning model are independent of this vision.

A conforming Persiqa implementation does not need natural-language processing
to construct or consume the CKM.

The future capability is an extension at the ingestion and authoring boundary,
not a change to the semantic foundation.

## 9. Future Evolution

If this capability moves into implementation scope, separate normative
specifications MAY later be introduced for:

- natural-language extraction;
- evidence and provenance;
- ambiguity handling;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- human validation workflows;
- extraction-to-PDS mapping;
- extraction conformance and evaluation.

Until then, this document remains a non-normative statement of future
direction.
