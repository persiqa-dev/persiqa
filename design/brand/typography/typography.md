# Persiqa Typography

## Primary Typeface

**Inter**

Use Inter across product UI, documentation, dashboards, diagrams, and supporting brand content.

## Weight hierarchy

- **Regular** — body text
- **Medium** — labels and navigation
- **Semibold** — headings and important emphasis
- **Bold** — rare strong emphasis

Typography should remain clean, restrained, highly readable, and subordinate to the geometric logo.

Exact type scale and line-height tokens remain an implementation task.
